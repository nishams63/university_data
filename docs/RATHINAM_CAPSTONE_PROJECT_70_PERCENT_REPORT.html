1. Executive Summary

Higher education governance at Rathinam Technical Campus depends on accurate operational reporting across four primary domains: biometric classroom attendance, continuous internal assessments (CIA), online LMS engagement, and corporate placement drives. In real-world educational infrastructure, edge IoT devices (biometric turnstiles), offline departmental portals, and retroactive approvals frequently deliver events hours or days out of order.

Traditional midnight-snapshot institutional systems permanently freeze reports at 23:59:59. Late events are either silently discarded or erroneously credited to the day of arrival, creating severe temporal distortions (e.g., student attendance falsely reported as absent on exam eligibility dates). This project delivers an engineered Stateful Watermark and Delta Recalculation Engine that automatically re-evaluates historical reports based on physical event timestamps, isolates high-impact modifications (≥ 15% drift or grade flips) into a human-in-the-loop review queue, and maintains an immutable versioned ledger with zero-loss compensating rollbacks.

2. Institutional Problem Statement & Objectives

Core Problem: Under naive baseline reporting, delayed events produce cumulative errors that escalate linearly with delay ratio. As verified in our benchmarks, an uncorrected 30% late-event stream generates a Mean Absolute Error of 117.25 and Root Mean Squared Error of 222.37, invalidating accreditation reports and NAAC compliance metrics.

Primary Objectives:

  • Temporal Decoupling: Derive institutional reporting date strictly from physical event timestamp ($t_E$) while tracking arrival timestamp ($t_A$).
  • Stateful Watermark: Enforce a bounded 24-hour lateness window ($\text{delay} > 24\text{h} \implies \text{LATE}$).
  • $O(1)$ Delta Recalculation: Incrementally apply payload metric contributions without re-scanning full historical tables.
  • Relational & Transactional Integrity: Enforce SQLite WAL mode, foreign keys, 10s busy timeouts, and single-session transaction boundaries.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Governance: Intercept alterations with relative impact ≥ 15% or high-risk outcome changes.

4. System Architecture & Relational Schema

The system follows a multi-tier decoupled architecture designed for institutional resilience and commodity edge hardware deployment:

Architectural Tiers:
  1. Ingestion & Validation Gateway: Deduplicates records on event_id primary key and validates domain schemas.
  2. Stateful Watermark & Delta Engine: Evaluates arrival latency against 24h watermark and computes exact contribution $\delta$.
  3. Atomic Transaction & Monotonic Version Ledger: Mutates raw_events, daily_reports, report_versions, and audit_logs within a single atomic SQLite WAL transaction.
  4. Human-in-the-Loop Review Queue: Intercepts updates where $\text{Drift} \ge 15\%$ or high-risk outcomes occur.
  5. Interactive React 18 Dashboard: Provides executive KPIs, version lineage explorer, and engine diagnostic monitoring.

Relational DDL & SQLite PRAGMA Specification

Table Name Primary Key Constraints & Foreign Keys Purpose & Indexing
raw_events event_id (VARCHAR) FK student_id → students Global idempotency log; indexed on (reporting_date, domain, is_valid).
daily_reports report_id (VARCHAR) UNIQUE(reporting_date, domain) Aggregates per date/domain; prevents duplicate reporting rows under concurrency.
report_versions id (AUTOINCREMENT) FK report_id (CASCADE), UNIQUE(report_id, version_number) Immutable version lineage ($v_1, v_2, \dots$); enforces monotonic ordering.
report_corrections correction_id (VARCHAR) FK report_id (CASCADE) Delta records, percentage impact, and review state machine.
audit_logs id (AUTOINCREMENT) UNIQUE(log_id) Append-only tamper-evident audit trail of all ingestion and rollback actions.
